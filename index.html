Privacy & Data Security Policy

SharePlate AP Platform Compliance & Protection Guidelines

1. Information We Collect & Use

SharePlate collects minimal information required to coordinate food redistribution effectively across Andhra Pradesh. This includes contact phone numbers, listing locations, and organization names provided voluntarily by food donors and verified NGO receivers. We never sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

2. Data Security & Encryption Standards

All communication channels and pickup coordination forms are secured with industry-standard TLS encryption. Donor phone numbers and exact pickup addresses are masked until a verified receiver confirms a secure pickup request, preventing unauthorized public scraping or spam.

3. Food Safety & Liability Compliance

In accordance with Indian Food Safety and Standards Authority (FSSAI) surplus food guidelines, donors attest that surplus meals are prepared under hygienic conditions. SharePlate acts strictly as a neutral technology bridge connecting donors with certified community volunteers and registered welfare shelters.

4. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You retain full control over your active listings and profile data. At any time, you can remove food listings or request complete account data purging by contacting our state support desk in Vijayawada.
